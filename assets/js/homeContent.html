REWARDS AT GAS PUMPS AND EVC STATIONS

The Rewarded Advertising (CRA) team has created and developed a special and unique Method of Marketing and Advertising (MMA) that will enable gasoline pumps and EVC stations, everywhere, to attract customers by giving away FREE CHANCES to play PICK-3 & PICK-4 instant number-guessing games that award BIG CA$H PRIZES. Players must first view 30-second video-ads, completely, in order to get "rewarded" with the FREE CHANCES.
Development of the MMA is sponsored by the Carnegie Mellon University in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, where the Swartz Center for Entrepreneurship provides its complete collaboration, facilities and full support.
PLAY OF PICK-3 & PICK-4 GAMES WILL NOT BE AVAILABLE ONLINE!!
30-second “rewarded video-ads” will be displayed on the touchscreen monitors of the participating gasoline pumps and EVC stations 24 hours a day. During the final five seconds of each 30-second video-ad, the following TEXT-LINK will appear in a corner of each video-ad, as shown in the "FUTURE SAMPLE VIDEO ADS" in the lower-left area of this page.
TAP FOR FREE PLAY
Whoever taps on the "TAP FOR FREE PLAY" text-link will be “rewarded” with a FREE CHANCE to play a game of PICK-3 or PICK-4 within the next 24 hours. Winners will receive BIG CA$H prizes instantly. There's a limit of five games per day.
THE REVENUE
Each complete view of a 30-second video-ad is worth $1.00 to the advertiser. Thousands of incomplete views are completely FREE for the advertiser.
A match of a four-digit number will occur once every 10,000 plays. If the prize for matching is set at $1,000.00, the prize-cost per play will average $0.10. A match of a three-digit number will occur once every 1,000 plays. If the prize for matching isset at $100.00, the prize-cost per play will also average $0.10.
For each complete view of a video-ad and tap of its text-link, advertisers will pay at least $1.00. The viewer will be rewarded with one FREE chance to play a Pick-3 or a Pick-4 game. Regardless of whicu game will be played, the net revenue for previously viewing its "companion" video-ad will be $0.90.
STATE LOTTERY PLAYERS
The PICK-3 & PICK-4 games are similar to state lottery Pick-3 & Pick-4 games, but the differences are substantial. State lottery Pick-3 & Pick-4 players must pay $1.00 to play and must wait hours for results. PICK-3 & PICK-4 players can play a new game FOR FREE every 30 seconds, 24/7, and win a CA$H prize instantly.
